Arcade in ES-DE: split or combine?

Use two folders: neogeo + fbneo. Avoid cps1/cps2/arcade for this list.

  • Default emulators decide it. On Android, ES-DE's cps1, cps2 and arcade systems default to MAME – Current, which is heavier on the G85 and needs MAME-version romsets. fbneo and neogeo default to the FinalBurn Neo core, so your games just launch.
  • Board-splitting leaves tiny systems. Of this list, CPS2 would hold two games and CPS1 none. Pang, Snow Bros, Klax and others aren't CPS anyway, so they'd still end up in fbneo.
  • Neo Geo is worth its own folder. It's about half the arcade list, and 320 px wide × 3 = exactly 960, so it scales pixel-perfect on the Block. CPS2 is 384 wide, and 3× overflows. Separate folders let you use RetroArch content-directory overrides for scaling and shaders, even though both run on the same core.
  • ES-DE sorts by the full game name (translated from the MAME short name, or scraped), so mixing boards in fbneo still lists cleanly A–Z.
  • Put neogeo.zip (BIOS) in RetroArch's system/ folder so both folders find it.
  • Optional: make an ES-DE custom collection (e.g. "Commute") that pulls your favourites across GBA, PS1 and arcade into one list.
