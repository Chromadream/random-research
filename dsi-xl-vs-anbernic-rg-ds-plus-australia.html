DSi XLvsRG DS Plus

Which second DS device to buy for standard dual-screen and touch games, when a DS Lite is already handling the hardware-gimmick titles like Hotel Dusk's lid puzzles, mic blowing and GBA-slot accessories.

Prices in AUDRG DS Plus launched 15 Sept 2026DSi XL launched 2009–10
Recommendation

Buy the Anbernic RG DS Plus

It costs about the same as a refurbished DSi XL here, shows DS games at a clean 4× integer scale on two 4.3" 1024×768 screens, and adds save states, upscaling, ROM library management and romhack support. A DSi XL adds very little your DS Lite doesn't already do.

  • Weak spots: capacitive touch is worse for handwriting, the body is large, charging is slow, and there are early QC reports.
  • Send handwriting-heavy games to the DS Lite along with the gimmick games.

When the DSi XL wins instead

Pick it if authentic resistive touch, instant sleep and resume, and real Nintendo hardware matter more to you than sharpness and convenience, or if you want DSiWare and DSi-enhanced features.

Refurbished units run about A$135–250 and are 15+ years old, so budget for a battery and inspect the hinge.

1024×768 is exactly four times 256×192 on each axis, so DS output maps with no uneven pixels and no letterbox. The DSi XL shows the original image on bigger, coarser pixels, which is why it looks soft up close. The RG DS Plus can also render 3D at 2× internally for most games.

Device profiles

The two candidates

Anbernic RG DS Plus

  • Price: US$94.99 after a US$84.99 72-hour preorder. Successor to the Android RG DS (Dec 2025).
  • Chip: Rockchip RK3568, 4× Cortex-A55 at 2.0 GHz, Mali-G52 2EE, 1 GB RAM.
  • OS: Linux stock OS boots from microSD 1; microSD 2 holds games (up to 2 TB). No internal storage.
  • Screens: Two 4.3" 1024×768 IPS at 60 Hz, OCA laminated. Only the bottom one is touch.
  • Touch: Capacitive with a short rubber-tip stylus stored in the right side.
  • Hinge: Alloy, stepless, holds 15°–180°. Hall sensor sleeps it on close.
  • Battery: 4000 mAh, ~6 h rated, 5V/1.5A charging.
  • Controls: D-pad, ABXY, four digital shoulders, one small analog stick, mic in the hinge.
  • Extras: Wi-Fi 5, BT 4.2, 3.5 mm jack, two USB-C, front stereo speakers. Matte Black, Coral Pink, Metallic Blue.

Nintendo DSi XL (refurbished)

  • Screens: Two 4.2" 256×192 panels. Sources disagree on IPS vs TFT, but angles beat the DS Lite. 18-bit colour.
  • Battery: 1050 mAh (UTL-003). Nintendo quotes ~13–17 h at lowest brightness, ~4–5 h at highest.
  • Common faults: Hinge wear and cracks, upper-screen ribbon stress, scratched screens, tired batteries.
  • Battery swap: iFixit rates it Easy, two Phillips screws. Aftermarket quality varies.
  • Region: DS carts are region-free; DSi-enhanced, DSi-exclusive and DSiWare titles are locked unless softmodded.
  • Softmod: Unlaunch plus TWiLight Menu++ runs ROMs from SD via nds-bootstrap and guards against most bricks. Minor brick risk on install.
  • Flashcarts: Needs DSi-compatible carts (R4i Gold 3DS Plus, DSTWO). Original R4s don't work.
  • No GBA slot: gbaRunner2 GBA emulation is unreliable on DSi hardware.
  • IPS mods: No drop-in laminated IPS kit found, unlike the DS Lite. USB-C mods exist.
Emulation on the RG DS Plus

How DS games actually run

Emulators. Stock firmware ships NNDDSS, Anbernic's closed-source DS emulator, reported to be based on neither melonDS nor DeSmuME. A DraStic RetroArch core is the fallback. ROCKNIX and GammaOS Nano both use DraStic. melonDS isn't realistic on the RK3568.

Performance. Every reviewer reports full speed across the DS library at native resolution, and most games handle 2× upscaling. A community patch fixed audio crackle in heavy 3D scenes.

Touch. The bottom 4:3 panel maps 1:1 to the DS touch screen. Good for tapping and dragging, weaker for fine handwriting.

Firmware options

  • Stock: Works out of the box with a DSi/3DS-style tile launcher. No RetroAchievements; box art needs a PC scraper.
  • ROCKNIX: Support merged into RK3566 nightlies at launch. Sleep, touch, both panels and both SD slots work. Audio fixes still landing late October; no stable release yet.
  • GammaOS Nano: Stripped Android. Built-in scraping and softcore RetroAchievements. Some beta stability glitches reported.

Reviews

What reviewers found

Time Extension8/10 · 1 Oct

Calls it the reviewer's new favourite way to play DS. Praises the D-pad and buttons, says touch registers almost every tap with slight latency, and measured around six hours per charge. Recommends GammaOS Nano.

Retro Handhelds28 Sept

One of the better clamshell D-pads, modelled on the DSi XL, and among the best dual-screen ergonomics, though wider than a DSi XL. Noted stylus delay and pressure marks on the top screen.

One of two review units died after charging and never came back.

Joey's Retro Handhelds23 Sept

Excellent DS performance; Pokémon Black 2 at full speed after updates. Measured about 3h45m of DS play and ~3 h to charge. Viewing angles shift between screens, and the size rules it out for DS Lite-size fans.

Junub Games8.1/10 · 16 Sept

A dozen DS games, all full speed. In Brain Age the real DSi read messy handwriting correctly while the RG DS Plus kept misreading numbers.

Retro Game Corpsshort post

Praised ROCKNIX nightly on the device: most games play well at 2× upscale with excellent input latency. Recommends swapping the stock stylus for a woven-tip one.

Store ratingslow signal

Anbernic's own store averages 4.86/5. Walmart's listing averages 3.3/5 from 6 ratings.

Plan

What to do next

  1. Buy the RG DS Plus

    Order direct from Anbernic (an Australian buyer reported delivery within a week) or via its eBay AU store for buyer protection. Take the base 16 GB model and add your own card.

  2. Test it inside the return window

    Check both screens, the hinge and charging, given Retro Handhelds' dead unit.

  3. Flash community firmware on a separate card

    ROCKNIX for the best latency and 2× DraStic, or GammaOS Nano for RetroAchievements and scraping. Keep the stock card as a fallback, and buy a woven-tip stylus.

  4. Route games by input

    Handwriting-heavy games (Brain Age, writing and precise drawing) go to the DS Lite, alongside the gimmick games.

If you go DSi XL anyway

Buy from EB Games (A$198) or Super Retro (12-month warranty) rather than as-is on eBay. Check the hinge and upper-screen ribbon, budget for a battery, and softmod with Unlaunch plus TWiLight Menu++.

Caveats

Limits of this research

  • RG DS Plus AUD prices are partly estimated. At ~1.42–1.43 AUD per USD, US$94.99 is ~A$135, or ~A$149 with GST, before unverified shipping. The eBay AU A$188–207 is confirmed, but postage inclusion isn't.
  • DSi XL prices are asking prices from active listings, not sold prices. Facebook Marketplace wasn't checked.
  • RG DS Plus battery figures conflict (~6 h claimed vs ~3h45m measured); expect 4–6 h. Some spec sites list wrong RAM, storage and battery figures.
  • No written Taki Udon review or substantive r/SBCGaming threads were found. Retro Game Corps' view is from a short post.
  • NNDDSS is new, closed-source software with unproven long-term development. Anbernic historically offers limited first-party updates.
