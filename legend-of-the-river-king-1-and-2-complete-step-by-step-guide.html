Story & Goal

Your sister Teruyo has fallen gravely ill. Your mother tells you the only cure is the legendary Guardian Fish, a magical fish hidden deep in the wilderness. Armed with a primitive fishing stick, you must journey through four regions, complete quests for the locals, gather better equipment, and ultimately catch the Guardian to save your sister.

How to Fish — Bait & Fly Fishing (Easier)

  1. Press and hold B to cast — the longer you hold, the farther you cast.
  2. The current carries your bobber. With fly fishing or a sinker, you can slowly reel in with A.
  3. When a fish shows interest, it circles your bobber and you'll hear three beeps.
  4. Then you'll hear one long beep — immediately press A or B to enter the underwater battle.
  5. Let the fish swim until it stops completely. Then hold A or B to reel it in.
  6. If the fish resists and you hear a loud screech, STOP holding the button immediately — otherwise the fish escapes.
  7. Repeat: wait → reel → release when it fights → wait → reel. Do this until caught.

How to Fish — Lure Fishing (Harder)

  1. Cast your lure out and pull it in (tap A/B rhythmically) so it acts like a living creature.
  2. When a fish approaches, stop moving the lure. It will circle.
  3. You'll hear multiple short beeps — press A or B to enter the underwater view.
  4. Drag the lure near the fish by holding A/B until the fish bites onto it.
  5. Once hooked, reel it in the same way as bait/fly fishing (let it tire, then reel).
  6. Lures can break during long battles — you'll need to buy replacements.
💡 Key Fishing Tip: The single most common mistake is reeling while the fish is still fighting. Always wait until the fish stops moving entirely before holding A/B. On the 3DS Virtual Console release, use Restore Points before each cast to avoid losing bait.

⚔️ Game 1 — Combat System

Random encounters with wild animals occur as you walk around. When you enter combat:

  • FIGHT — A fist moves randomly on screen. Press A when the fist reaches the animal's weak point for maximum damage.
  • MEAL — Eat food to recover HP mid-battle.
  • RUN — Attempt to flee (often fails; usually better to fight).

Damage dealt scales with your current HP. More HP = harder hits. More EXP = higher max HP.

🏆 Game 1 — Best Equipment & Money Strategy

💰 How to Get Rich Fast

Once you reach the Stream area, catch Ayu (use Stonefly Wet fly or Spinner lure) and sell them to the Uncle by the Restaurant for 60 Gold each — far more than the standard fish market pays. This is the best farming method in the game. Fish one side of the river, then switch sides, and enter/exit a building to respawn all fish.

🗺️ Game 1 — Complete Step-by-Step Walkthrough

🏘️ Area 1: TORRENT (Hometown)

1 Wake Up & Get Started

  • Your mother wakes you — sister Teruyo is sick. Mother gives you a Charm.
  • Go outside. Catch some fish with your starter rod + worms and sell them for early cash.
  • Talk to everyone in town to learn about the world.

2 Get the Raft from the Raft Master

  • Talk to the Raft Master. He wants a Rainbow Trout.
  • Catch Rainbow Trout east of the bridge using Larva + Medium Hook.
  • Give him the Rainbow Trout. He'll build you a raft.

3 Cutthroat Trout for Lunch

  • The Raft Master's niece (in the house across the bridge) needs a Cutthroat Trout.
  • Go as far west as possible. Use Larva + Small or Medium Hook.
  • Bring the fish back to the niece, then deliver the lunch to the Raft Master.

4 Torrent Wrap-Up (Grind Phase)

  • Now you can raft into deeper waters. Rafting costs HP — be careful!
  • Catch at least one of every fish in Torrent (Bullhead, Char, Cutthroat Trout, Gila Trout, Rainbow Trout, Trout, Goby, B.S.Brm).
  • Buy the Net (100G) and Pail Medium (100G).
  • Save up and buy all rods and the Stonefly Wet fly.
  • Target: Have at least 1,000 Gold before leaving Torrent.
  • Fight every animal — HP growth from EXP is crucial.
⚠️ Don't skip the grind: Having good equipment and lots of gold now makes the rest of the game much smoother.

🌊 Area 2: LAKE 1

5 Broken Raft

  • Your raft crashes into a rock upon entering Lake 1. The Pupil (Raft Master's student) offers to fix it for free. Explore while you wait.

6 Trade the Charm

  • Talk to the Artist. Trade your Charm → Teacup.
  • You can't use the Teacup yet, but it's part of a critical trade chain.

7 Defeat the Bear (Boss)

  • A bear is terrorizing the town. Stock up on Rice Balls.
  • Walk south across the bridges. You'll encounter the Bear (HP: 68, WP: Face).
  • By now you should have enough EXP to beat it in 4–5 hits on its face.
  • Return. The Pupil says the old raft was destroyed but he built you a new one.

8 Get the Pickaxe

  • Go to the Restaurant in the southeast corner. The Miner wants Three-Spined Stickleback.
  • Found in reeds next to the village. Use Stonefly Wet or Mayfly Wet/Dry.
  • Give him the fish → receive Pick.

9 Find the Secret Cave (Optional but Helpful)

  • Talk to the Map Maker (north of Miner's house). He wants Smelt.
  • Use Stonefly Wet near the bridges. Give him one → he reveals the cave location.

10 Lake 1 Wrap-Up

  • Catch every fish species in Lake 1 (Black Bass, Blue Gill, Brown Trout, Darter, Golden Trout, Lake Trout, Minnow, Smelt, Three-Spined Stickleback, Blowfish, Sea Bass).
  • Have at least 900 Gold. Stock Rice Balls. Use the Pick on the cave to enter the Stream.

🏞️ Area 3: STREAM

11 Save the Drowning Man

  • Exit the cave into a new town. A man is drowning in the water — save him.
  • He gives you the Casting Rod, Small as thanks.

12 Get the Charcoal

  • Go southeast to the Restaurant. Trade your Teacup → Charcoal.

13 Quick Cash: Sell Ayu

  • Catch Ayu (use Stonefly Wet). Sell to the Uncle near the Restaurant for 60G each.
  • This is your best money farm for the whole game!

14 Open the Dam

  • Further downstream, a girl wants a big Pale Chub (must break a record to qualify).
  • Use Stonefly Wet or Larva + Small Hook near the Teacup trade area.
  • She's the Dam Master's daughter — she'll have him open the dam for Lake 2.

15 Stream Wrap-Up

  • Catch all fish except Golden Carp and Bitterling (can't reach those yet).
  • Buy the Pail Large (200G). Have at least 900 Gold.

🏔️ Area 4: LAKE 2

16 New Equipment

  • You're now in the best area for big fish like Eels. Head to the town in the northeast.
  • Buy the Pail Large if you haven't yet. Lunches are now available.

17 Get the Axe

  • On the western side, an old Lumberjack will trade his Axe for a Willow Gudgeon and a Slender Gudgeon.
  • Both respond well to Stonefly Wet + Small Hook.
  • Willow Gudgeon: fish off the southern side of the lower western peninsula.
  • Slender Gudgeon: fish in the small pond west of the short bridge near town.

18 Get the Wind Chime (Trade Chain Finale)

  • The lady in the Restaurant won't trade yet — you must help her family first:
  • ① Go north of town to a man on a dock who wants Killifish. Catch 5 → receive Lure Rod, Large.
  • ② Go back to the Stream and help the man near the Ayu-buying Uncle.
  • ③ Back in Lake 2, help the man near the cliff.
  • ④ Now the lady will trade: Charcoal → Wind Chime.

19 Lake 2 Wrap-Up

  • You should now have: All rods, all lures/baits/flies, all hooks, Pail Large, Axe, Wind Chime, and all fish except Guardian, Bitterling, and Common Carp. Target 1,000+ Gold.

🏁 FINISHING THE GAME

20 The Secret Pond — Catch the Guardian Fish!

  1. Return to the Stream. Find a lone tree (with one stone above and one below it) on the western landmass.
  2. Use the Axe on the tree to open a path to the secret pond.
  3. In the pond, catch a Bitterling (Small Hook + Worm) and a Common Carp (Stonefly Wet).
  4. Equip a Large Hook + Dough Ball (this is the ONLY bait the Guardian eats).
  5. Use the Wind Chime to summon the Guardian Fish.
  6. Cast directly into the middle of the pond. The Guardian (85–106 cm) isn't too hard to reel in.
✅ Game Complete! After catching the Guardian, you return home, your mother cures Teruyo, and the credits roll. The game will tell you if you've unlocked Fun Lake.

🏝️ Game 1 — Fun Lake & Raise Mode

Fun Lake (Secret 5th Area)

  • Unlock: Catch all 48 fish (including the Guardian). A message appears after the credits.
  • Access: Sleep at the Inn in Torrent — you'll wake up in Fun Lake.
  • Contents: One of every fish in the game + an Inn (10G). No enemies here.
  • Note: Records don't get recorded in your Fishing Memo here, but you can keep and sell fish.
  • To leave: Sleep at the Inn in Fun Lake.

Raise Mode (Virtual Pet Minigame)

A Tamagotchi-style minigame where fish evolve through 6 stages based on what you feed them. Example evolution chain: Three-Spined Stickleback → Sculpin (40% worm) → Dark Chub (70% larva) → Blue Gill (20% worm + 20% larva) → Black Bass (25% worm + 25% larva + 25% small fish + 10% frog) → Rainbow Trout.

🔧 Game 1 — Secrets, Cheats & GameShark Codes

In-Game Secrets

  • Music While Fishing: While fishing, press A to open the menu → Tools → Memo → Fishing Record. Exit — music now plays while fishing.
  • Infinite Free Bait Trick: Use the Net to catch bait. Move one space away, then back — more bait will have spawned.
  • Fish Respawn: Enter any building and exit — all fish return to their original spots (no need to buy Chumming for this).

Reeling In (Underwater Scene)

  1. Let the fish swim away until it stops completely.
  2. Hold A or B to reel it in.
  3. When the fish arches up and stops moving toward you, RELEASE the button or it will escape.
  4. Repeat until the fish is reeled to the top of the screen.

Lure-specific: In the underwater scene with lures/trolling flies, the fish ISN'T hooked yet. You must press A to drag the lure near the fish until it bites. Don't move it too fast — some fish are slow swimmers.

Other Activities

🐚 Shellfish Diving

  • Available in Beach, Shore, Ocean, Remote Island. Need canoe/boat + Flippers (200G). Press B repeatedly to swim down. Avoid eels, jellyfish, lanternfish. Reach the bottom to auto-collect.

🦗 Bug Collecting

  • Press A → menu → BUG. Use Tree Sap (20G) to freeze moving bugs. Must wait for ground/tree bugs to stop moving before catching. Flying bugs require positioning cursor in flight path.

🌸 Flower Collecting

  • Walk up to pink flower graphics. Press A → menu → PIC. Only the front flower can be picked. Flowers regrow after day→night or night→day transitions.

Combat (Same as Game 1 but with new enemies)

  • Random encounters. Options: BEAT (aim fist at weak point), EAT (use food), RUN (may fail).
  • Enemies can steal your fish!
  • HP at time of attack determines damage output.

🗺️ Game 2 — Kid Walkthrough (Start at The Lake)

Note: The Kid starts at Level 1 with 50 HP — the harder route. If you're struggling, consider restarting as the Teen (Level 8, 80 HP).

🌿 The Lake

  1. Get the Watch: Man by Tackle Shop wants a Roach (south of Inn). Use starting equipment. Reward: Watch (change time of day).
  2. Get the Canoe: Man by Inn wants a Sucker Eel (north of bridge among water plants). Use Small Hook + worms. Reward: Canoe.
  3. +10 HP Bonus: Old lady across bridge wants 5 Green Grasshoppers (found just north of her). Reward: +10 max HP.
  4. Get Tofu to pass the Ogre: Foxes in southwest house need a Pink flower (on island in middle of lake — use canoe). Give Pink → receive Tofu. Give Tofu to ogre blocking path → access to The Stream.

🏞️ The Stream

  1. 8 ft Lure Rod: Girl by Inn is tired of Roses. Pick a Violet (by save shrine) → give to girl → receive Rose. Give Rose to the guy behind her → receive 8 ft Lure Rod.
  2. Get the Axe (Bridge to Summit): Worker near entrance lost his Axe in the pond. Catch a Carp (south of waterfall — use Fly or Lure rod; Carp won't take bait-hook). Give Carp to the girl at the pond → she becomes the Goddess → receive Axe. Give Axe to worker → bridge to Summit built.

⛰️ The Summit

  1. Hyacinth: Boy left of bridge wants a Blue Gill. Catch one just right of the bridge. Reward: Hyacinth (S-Rank flower).
  2. 14 ft Lure Rod: Fisherman on southern island needs 3 Breams (west of bridge). Reward: 14 ft Lure Rod.
  3. 5 ft Fly Rod: Father-son duo in north want a Catfish (found just south of them). Reward: 5 ft Fly Rod.
  4. Talk to Yuki: She's on rocks at the waterfall. River King is here but won't appear until it rains. She sends you to the Underwater Palace for a Cloud Seed Slug. You now need to reach the sea maps.

🏖️ The Beach (Kid's First Visit to Sea Maps)

  1. Small Surf Rod: Man east of Tackle Shop wants Opaleye. Catch just south of him. Reward: Small Surf Rod.
  2. Tulips for Cash: Girl north of Fish Market exchanges Kasago or Sea Sculpin for S-Rank Tulips (50G each!). Farm these for money.
  3. Save the Turtle: Boy pestering a turtle wants 300 Gold (he says "3 dollars" — translation error!). Pay him → Turtle invites you to the Sea Palace.

🌊 The Shore

  1. Get Dad's Boat: Canoe can't handle Shore waters. Dad wants a Pearl Oyster. Dive in northeast corner of Beach (need Flippers!). Return it → access to Dad's Boat.
  2. Find the Turtle: Boat far east, then slightly north. Dive next to Turtle — need ~140 HP to survive the deep swim. Reach the Underwater Palace.
  3. Princess needs Cloud Sea Slug: Found diving along Beach coastline (near the lady who mentions "strange creature"). Give to Princess → receive Cloud Seed Slug box. Return to Yuki at Summit.

🔙 Back to The Stream → The Moor

  1. Get the Pickax: Worker is now in northwest corner. He gives back the Axe. Go to Goddess pond, drop Axe → get Pickax. Give to worker → path to The Moor opened.

🌲 The Moor

  1. 11 ft Fly Rod: Lady by Inn wants Brook Trout (far southern waters). Reward: 11 ft Fly Rod.
  2. 7 Spring Herbs (+40 HP): Girl at home wants: Bee Nettle, Chickweed, Cottonweed, Parsley, Radish, Shepherd's Purse, Turnip. The girl by Fish Market tells you which. Reward: +40 max HP!
  3. Get Pansy: Boy north of bridge wants a Cherry Salmon (northeast of him). Reward: Pansy (S-Rank).
  4. Get Cucumber: Girl on east side of river (south end) wants Rainbow Trout (south of bridge — use Fly/Lure, not bait). Reward: Cucumber.
  5. Visit Kappa (Master Craftsman): Locked door in northwest corner. Give Cucumber → Kappa lets you in. He needs Genji Firefly + Heike Firefly (found east of his cave by firefly boy). Give him your Frog Lure → he modifies it for the River King.

⛰️ Return to The Summit — Catch the River King!

  1. Brown Trout Contest: Yuki's dad (Zeus) demands a contest. Catch a Brown Trout at least 65 cm (near waterfall, use Lure/Fly). Beat Yuki → she makes it rain.
  2. Catch the River King: Northwest corner among Snakeheads. Use 14 ft Lure Rod + Kappa's Frog Lure. Darker fins than normal Snakeheads. Once caught → half the Heaven Jewel restored.

🌊 Now Chase the Sea King (Brother Failed)

  1. Calm the Storm: Dad's boat to northeast Shore → storm appears. Dad needs a "Scarab" — get a Dung Beetle from Stream's southwest corner.
  2. 30b Trolling Rod: Man south of Shore dock wants "Red Seabream" (mislabeled as "S. Reabrm" — translation glitch!). Found around the middle island or lighthouse in Ocean.
  3. Ocean — Small Boat Rod: Man south of Tackle Shop wants Yellowtail (west of lighthouse in northeast corner).
  4. Save the Girl: Little girl at dock attacked by Sea Eagle. Defeat it → receive Momo (Stuffed Doll).
  5. Get the Cruiser: Captain can't sail while mermaids sing. Get S. Cicada from behind Stream's Fish Market.
  6. Visit Mermaids: Cruiser to shipwreck in southeast Ocean corner. Dive down. Mermaid wants 2 Scallops (dive south of lighthouse). She tells you about an old man on a Shore island.
  7. 130b Trolling Rod: Shore's southeast island cave. Old man recognizes Momo. Beat his contest: catch Spearfish ≥ 230 cm (just north of his island). Reward: 130b Trolling Rod.
  8. Catch the Sea King: Equip 130b Trolling Rod + Squid fly. Go to shipwreck. Sea King hides among Bluefin Tuna northwest of the wreck.
✅ Both Kings Caught! Yuki reassembles the Heaven Jewel. You notice an island inside it…

🏝️ Game 2 — Remote Island & The King of Kings

Accessing the Remote Island

  1. After catching both Kings, the credits roll. Reload your save — you'll be at your house.
  2. Dad tells you to talk to the cruiser captain at The Shore.
  3. The captain takes you to the Remote Island — Yuki's home among the gods.

Catching the King of Kings (True Final Boss)

  1. Yuki's in trouble again (stole a star from the Milky Way). Zeus will forgive her if you catch him in fish form.
  2. Requirement: Complete the Fish Memo — all 103 fish documented. (The King of Kings is #104.)
  3. Rock Cod is a commonly missed fish — found along the east side of The Beach.
  4. 6 new fish appear at the Remote Island (Alowana, Amiya, Itou Salmon, King Salmon, Tilapia, Yellow Perch). They're all between the northern bridge and Yuki's cliff. Species change randomly — use the Watch to switch day/night until all 6 appear.
  5. Once you have all 103, talk to Yuki → King of Kings appears as a King Salmon at the base of the cliff.
  6. Catch him with your Lure Rod + Frog Lure.
👑 TRUE ENDING! Zeus forgives Yuki. You've beaten the whole game!

🔗 Game 2 — Trading with Harvest Moon 2 & River King 2

Trading Between Two River King 2 Carts

  • Use Gamelink Cable. Both players go to Grocery Shop → talk to old man → Comm.
  • Trade fish/shellfish for fish/shellfish, bugs for bugs, flowers for flowers.

Trading with Harvest Moon 2 (GBC)

  • River King 2 player: Grocery Shop → Comm.
  • Harvest Moon 2 player: Mary's Library → Communication Center (closed Sundays/event days).
  • To complete Flower Memo you MUST trade with HM2 for: Camomile, Sage, Saffron, Rosemary.
  • To complete Bug Memo you need from HM2: H. Beetle, M. Butterfly, O.S. Beetle.

🐛 Game 2 — Known Glitches & Translation Errors

  • "S. Reabrm" = Red Seabream: Man at Shore dock asks for "S. Reabrm" — this is a mistranslation. He actually wants a Red Seabream.
  • "3 dollars" = 300 Gold: The boy harassing the turtle says "3 dollars" but actually demands 300 Gold.
  • "Cow" = Crow: Summit enemy labeled "Cow" is actually a bird (Crow).
  • "Tale" = Tail: The game manual says to hit a Seal on its "Tale." It should be "Tail."
  • Rockfish mislabel: The manual says Rockfish are at The Beach. They're actually called Kasago in-game. The Tulip girl won't accept actual Rockfish — only Kasago or Sea Sculpin.
  • Cherry Trout = Cherry Salmon: Moor map in manual lists "Cherry Trout" — it's actually Cherry Salmon.
  • Ocean Fish Market pricing: The $ sign appears but the gold amount is missing from the dialogue.
  • Rumble Pack: Removed from the English release.

🎯 Quick-Reference: Which Game Should You Play?

  • Play River King 1 if: You want a focused, shorter fishing RPG with a sweet story about saving your sister. The combat is simpler, the fishing mechanics are more forgiving, and there's less to collect.
  • Play River King 2 if: You want a much larger game with two story paths, ocean fishing, shellfish diving, bug/flower collecting, trading, and a massive 104-fish memo to complete. The scope is dramatically bigger.
  • Play both in order: River King 1 teaches the core mechanics. River King 2 expands on everything and adds sea fishing. The original also has the charming Raise Mode virtual pet feature that the sequel lacks.

⚠️ Common Beginner Mistakes in Both Games

  • Reeling while the fish fights: You MUST wait for the fish to stop moving completely or it escapes.
  • Wrong hook size: Small fish need Small Hooks, large fish need Large Hooks. Using the wrong size = guaranteed escape.
  • Not buying the Net early (Game 1): Free bait saves tons of gold.
  • Not buying Flippers (Game 2): You can't dive effectively without them.
  • Skipping enemy fights: HP growth from EXP is essential for longer casts and survival.
  • Rafting/running everywhere: Movement costs HP. Take efficient routes and use the Inn.
  • Not catching all fish in each area before moving on: Makes 100% completion much harder later.

💡 Top 10 Tips for Both Games

  1. Stonefly Wet (Game 1) / Spinner Bait (Game 2) are your go-to all-purpose lures/flies.
  2. Always carry at least 5–10 food items before exploring new areas.
  3. Talk to every NPC — many give quests, items, or money-making opportunities.
  4. Entering/exiting any building respawns all fish — no need for Chumming/Ground Bait.
  5. In Game 1, grind the Torrent thoroughly before moving on. Buy the Fly Rod Large + Stonefly Wet.
  6. In Game 2, the Teen is easier for beginners (starts at Level 8 vs Kid's Level 1).
  7. Use the Watch (Game 2) to toggle day/night — some fish/bugs only appear at specific times.
  8. In Game 2, don't sell S-Rank collectibles unless you've already traded them to complete memos.
  9. The Dough Ball in Game 1 is ONLY for the Guardian Fish — don't use it for anything else.
  10. On 3DS Virtual Console, use Restore Points liberally — these games are punishing.
