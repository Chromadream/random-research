Setup and scope

  • Firmware: use GammaOS 1.4.4 or later. It fixes touch not reaching the whole screen on the Plus and includes general microphone fixes.
  • Scaling: each 1024×768 panel is exactly 4× the DS's 256×192.
  • Capacitive: taps and drags should translate fine. None of the tap and drag games here have been tested on the Plus, so try a few minutes first.
  • DSiWare left out: as far as I know, DraStic doesn't run it, which rules out the Art Style puzzlers.
  • Left out on purpose: Shin Megami Tensei and Devil Survivor (better on your 3DS), and Fire Emblem (covered by your separate series run).

Keep these on the DS Lite

  • Hotel Dusk, Last Window, Trace Memory: fold-the-console puzzles, and Hotel Dusk has a two-point touch puzzle.
  • Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story: giant battles need the stylus and mic with the DS held vertically.
  • The World Ends With You: frantic stylus combat.
  • Meteos: a fast-drag game whose button controls are too slow to be useful.
  • Phantom Hourglass and Spirit Tracks: fully stylus-driven, with lid and mic moments.
