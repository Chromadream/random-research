A March 2024 No-Intro DAT contains 2,104 retail games. Its untrimmed card images total 2.055 TB.[8]

The current CDN DAT has 10,152 records. This number includes updates, DLC, and other content, so it is not a game count.[9]

Use decrypted CCI files for retail games. Use one CIA or CDN form for each digital game.

Azahar supports compressed ZCCI and ZCIA files. Standard ZIP and 7z files can require extraction before use.[10]

CAUTION: Trimming or compression changes the full-file hash. Keep untrimmed images only if DAT-level preservation is necessary.

Untrimmed 3DS masters can add 2 TB or more. A 24 TB disk then has too little free space.

Operation

  • Use ext4 on the data disk.
  • Use Samba over wired Gigabit Ethernet.
  • Enable SMART tests and temperature alerts.
  • Keep verified source files read-only.
  • Keep patches and modified games in a separate folder.
  • Keep saves on another device and at an off-site location.

Gigabit Ethernet has a theoretical limit of 125 MB/s.[15] A 50 Mb/s video uses 6.25 MB/s.

Use Direct Play when possible. Video conversion starts when the client does not support the source format.[16]

Each continuous 10 W costs about $23-$29 per year at the sampled Australian tariffs.[17]

Appendix: RAID 1 cost

RAID 1 writes the same data to two drives. One drive can fail without stopping access to the files.

Two 24 TB drives give 24 TB of usable space. RAID 1 does not give 48 TB of usable space.

An mdadm RAID 1 or a ZFS mirror has the same drive cost. Both software options are free.
