4. Subwoofer for 2.1

For a small room, a sealed subwoofer integrates most naturally for music and avoids boomy bass. The SVS SB-1000 Pro is the gold standard at this price point.

SVS SB-1000 Pro

AUD $1,214–1,349

Available at: Audio Trends, West Coast HiFi, Eastwood HiFi, LUMI-FI

The SB-1000 Pro has speaker-level inputs, which is crucial if using the Schiit split stack (since the Saga 2 has no dedicated sub out). You can run speaker wire from the Gjallarhorn F's outputs to the sub's speaker-level inputs, and the sub's internal crossover handles bass management.

With the WiiM Ultra or Bluesound Node, you'd use the dedicated subwoofer RCA output with proper bass management — a cleaner approach.

6. Important Considerations

⚠️ Gjallarhorn F Power Limitations

10W into 8Ω is genuinely low by modern standards. This amp is designed for:

  • Desktop/nearfield — sitting close to the speakers
  • Small rooms — bedrooms, studies, small living rooms
  • Efficient speakers — 88dB+ sensitivity strongly recommended
  • Moderate volumes — not party levels

If your room is on the larger side of "small," or you like to listen loud, consider stepping up to the Schiit Vidar 2F (USD $799, ~AUD $1,300–1,500 est.) for 100W into 8Ω — much more headroom.

🎛️ Volume Control

The Gjallarhorn F is a pure power amplifier — it has no volume knob. You must have a preamp or DAC with volume control. All options in this guide include volume control (WiiM Ultra, Bluesound Node, Saga 2, Fosi ZD3 all function as preamps).

📺 HDMI ARC vs Optical

  • HDMI ARC: TV remote controls volume. Supports CEC (auto on/off). Limited to stereo PCM or Dolby Digital (which is fine for 2.1).
  • Optical: Same audio quality as HDMI ARC for stereo. No CEC. Needs an HDMI ARC extractor if your TV only has HDMI ARC output (most modern TVs do). Many TVs still have optical out — check yours.
  • If your TV has optical out: You can skip the extractor entirely and go TV optical → Modi+ (or WiiM Ultra optical in). This simplifies the Schiit stack significantly.

🍎 Apple TV as Streamer

The Apple TV 4K is a capable streamer for Apple Music, Spotify, Tidal, etc. via the TV's HDMI ARC. However, for critical music listening, a dedicated streamer with a better DAC (like the WiiM Ultra or Bluesound Node) will sound noticeably better than the TV's internal DAC. With the Schiit stack, you'd be relying on the Apple TV → TV → optical → Modi+ path, which is perfectly fine but not as elegant as an all-in-one solution.

🔮 Future Expansion

  • CD Player: Any CD player with optical or coaxial output can connect to the WiiM Ultra (optical in), Bluesound Node (optical in), or Modi+ (optical/coax). The Saga 2 has 4 RCA inputs for analog sources.
  • Dedicated Streamer: With the WiiM Ultra or Bluesound Node, streaming is already built-in. With the Schiit stack, you'd add a WiiM Mini ($139) or Pro ($219–339) via optical into the Modi+.
  • Turntable: The WiiM Ultra has a built-in MM phono stage. For the Schiit stack, add a Schiit Mani ($149 USD, ~$250 AUD est.) → Saga 2.
  • Mono-blocking: The Saga 2's balanced XLR outputs let you run two Gjallarhorn Fs in mono (30W each). A future upgrade path for more power.

📏 Room Size & Speaker Placement

For a small room (say, 3m × 4m or smaller):

  • Place speakers at least 30cm from the rear wall (especially rear-ported designs like the Diamond 12.1 and 3030i).
  • Form an equilateral triangle with your listening position.
  • Tweeters at ear height.
  • If using a subwoofer, do the "subwoofer crawl" to find the best position.
  • The WiiM Ultra's room correction can help tame room modes.
