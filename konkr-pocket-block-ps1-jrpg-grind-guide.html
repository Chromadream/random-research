Setup on the Block

  • Folder: psx. Convert discs to .chd to save space; for multi-disc games, put the CHDs in a .m3u playlist so ES-DE shows one entry and the emulator can swap discs.
  • Speed-up: the G85 has headroom for fast-forward on PS1, which is most of what makes grinding bearable. Map it to a shoulder or hotkey.
  • Scaling: most PS1 games are 240 lines, so 3× = 720 exactly fills the screen height. Some games switch to 480i for menus or FMV; that still fits at 1.5×.
  • D-pad only: none of these need analog sticks.
  • Fan translations: marked where the game never came out in English.
