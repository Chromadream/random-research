Steam notes

  • Skip the 2014 and 2015 Steam versions of the original Trails in the Sky FC and SC. The 1st and 2nd Chapter remakes replace them.
  • Save carry-over between games reads from your local or Steam Cloud save folder, so keep each sequel in the same Steam library as the game before it.
  • The Cold Steel games have a turbo mode that speeds up battles and walking, which helps with grinding and replays.
