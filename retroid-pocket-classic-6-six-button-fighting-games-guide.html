Setup on the Pocket Classic 6

  • Why this device: the Classic 6 has Saturn-style face buttons, three on top and three below. Put punches on the top row (light → heavy, left to right) and kicks on the bottom row, matching an arcade panel. Check in RetroArch's input settings what the C and Z buttons report as before mapping.
  • Folder: everything here goes in fbneo so it runs on FinalBurn Neo. ES-DE's cps1/cps2/cps3 systems default to MAME – Current on Android.
  • Scaling on the 1240×1080 screen: the CPS boards and Kaiser Knuckle (Taito F3, 232 lines) get 4× vertically: 224 lines × 4 = 896 tall and about 1195 wide at 4:3, which fits. The Midway games are about 254 lines; 4× would need roughly 1355 px of width at 4:3, so use 3× (about 762 tall).
  • CPS3: the Snapdragon G1 Gen 2 has much more headroom than the Block's G85, so this is the better device for 3rd Strike.
  • The 3- and 4-button games (Neo Geo, Pocket Fighter, Cyberbots, JoJo) are on the Block list.
