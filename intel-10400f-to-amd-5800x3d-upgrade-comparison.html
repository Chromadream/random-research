🎂 The 5800X3D 10th Anniversary Edition

What Is It?

AMD re-released the legendary 5800X3D in June 2026 to celebrate 10 years of the AM4 platform. It's the same silicon as the original (same 8C/16T, 3.4/4.5 GHz, 96MB L3), but manufactured using AMD's newer 2nd-gen 3D V-Cache bonding process because the original production line was retired.

The Anniversary Edition includes commemorative packaging and a Carbice® Ice Pad™ thermal interface material in the box. No cooler included.

💸 Why DDR5 Is Currently Unsustainable

The "RAMageddon" of 2025–2026: AI data centres are consuming an estimated 70% of all high-end DRAM production. Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron have prioritised server/HBM memory over consumer DDR5 because the margins are far higher. The result? Consumer DDR5 has become scarce and extremely expensive.

📡 WiFi 7 — Can You Add It to an ITX Build?

The Honest Answer: Not easily with ITX. All AM4 ITX motherboards come with WiFi 6 (802.11ax) onboard — none have WiFi 7 built in. Since the single PCIe x16 slot is occupied by your 9060XT GPU, you cannot install a PCIe WiFi 7 card (like the ASUS PCE-BE6500 at ~$89 AUD or MSI HERALD BE6500 at ~$59 AUD).

🔧 Workarounds for WiFi 7 on ITX

OptionViabilityDetails
USB WiFi 7 Adapter Viable USB 3.2 WiFi 7 adapters exist and can plug into rear or front USB ports. Performance won't match PCIe but is the simplest solution. Expect ~$60–120 AUD when available.
M.2 WiFi Card Swap Advanced B550I boards use an M.2 E-key slot for the WiFi/BT module (hidden under the VRM/I/O shroud). You could theoretically swap the Intel AX200 for an Intel BE200 (WiFi 7) module — but this requires disassembly, BIOS support, and is not guaranteed to work with B550's older platform.
Stick with WiFi 6 Recommended WiFi 6 (802.11ax) is still excellent — up to 2.4 Gbps theoretical, MU-MIMO, OFDMA. For most users, WiFi 6 is more than sufficient. The 6 GHz band (WiFi 6E/7) requires a compatible router anyway.
Use 2.5GbE Wired Best All recommended B550I boards include 2.5GbE LAN. If you can run Ethernet to your ITX build, you'll get better latency and stability than any WiFi solution.

💡 Recommendation: The onboard Intel AX200 WiFi 6 on both the Gigabyte and ASUS B550I boards is very capable. Pair it with a good WiFi 6 router and you'll likely never notice the difference vs WiFi 7 for gaming. The 2.5GbE port is your best friend if Ethernet is available.

🎮 GPU Clearance: 9060XT + ITX

Your 9060XT is a dual-slot PCIe x16 card. On an ITX board, the single PCIe x16 slot is positioned at the edge — your GPU will fit fine mechanically. Just ensure your ITX case has enough GPU length clearance for the 9060XT.

  • PCIe version: The 5800X3D + B550 chipset supports PCIe 4.0, so your GPU runs at full bandwidth (up from PCIe 3.0 on the 10400F).
  • Resizable BAR (Smart Access Memory): Supported on the 5800X3D + B550 combo, which can boost GPU performance in supported games.
  • No slot interference: The GPU occupies the only PCIe slot. All M.2, SATA, and USB connectivity is via onboard headers — no conflicts.

🌡️ Cooling & PSU Considerations

Cooler Requirements

  • The 5800X3D has a 105W TDP (vs 65W on the 10400F)
  • No stock cooler included — you'll need an aftermarket cooler
  • Recommended: Mid-range tower air cooler (e.g., Noctua NH-D15, be quiet! Dark Rock Pro 4) or a 240mm AIO liquid cooler
  • Max operating temp: 90°C (lower than 10400F's 100°C)
  • The included Carbice Ice Pad helps with thermal transfer
  • The 5800X3D cannot be traditionally overclocked (multiplier locked to protect 3D V-Cache)
  • In ITX cases, airflow is limited — consider undervolting via PBO2 Curve Optimizer for better temps

PSU & Other Notes

  • Your existing PSU is likely fine — the 5800X3D draws ~105W (142W PPT), which with a 9060XT should be manageable on a 550–650W+ PSU
  • BIOS update required: B550I boards need BIOS version supporting Ryzen 5000. Both Gigabyte (Q-Flash Plus) and ASUS (BIOS FlashBack) support CPU-less flashing
  • RAM sweet spot: DDR4-3600 with tight timings (CL16 or CL14) is optimal for the 5800X3D
  • Your existing DDR4 will work, but if it's DDR4-2666, consider upgrading to DDR4-3200 or 3600 for best results

✅ Pros of Upgrading

  • Massive gaming uplift: 50–60%+ FPS gains in CPU-bound games
  • Keep your DDR4: No need to buy overpriced DDR5
  • PCIe 4.0: Full bandwidth for your 9060XT and NVMe SSD
  • 8 cores / 16 threads: Better multitasking, streaming, productivity
  • 3D V-Cache: Game-changing for cache-sensitive titles
  • ~$500–700 cheaper than going AM5 ITX + DDR5
  • AM4 is mature and stable: BIOS/drivers are battle-tested
  • 10th Anniversary extras: Carbice Ice Pad included

⚠️ Cons / Considerations

  • Dead-end platform: AM4 will receive no new CPUs. This is your final upgrade on this socket
  • No overclocking: The 3D V-Cache locks the multiplier
  • 105W TDP: Runs hotter than your 65W 10400F — need adequate cooling
  • $549 AUD for a 4-year-old architecture: It's a premium price for old(er) silicon, even if justified by the market
  • DDR4 bandwidth ceiling: In some edge cases (RT-enabled Spider-Man 2), DDR4 bandwidth limits performance vs DDR5 systems
  • No WiFi 7 onboard: Stuck with WiFi 6 unless you use USB/M.2 workarounds
  • No integrated graphics: Need your GPU for any display output
  • Future-proofing: AM5 will get future CPU upgrades; AM4 won't

💡 Key Insights You Didn't Ask About (But Should Know)

🔌 BIOS Update Is Mandatory

B550I boards may ship with an older BIOS that doesn't support the 5800X3D. Both the Gigabyte (Q-Flash Plus) and ASUS (BIOS FlashBack) boards can be updated via USB without a CPU installed. Don't skip this step.

🔊 Audio Quality Upgrade

Both B550I boards feature premium audio codecs (ALC1220-VB on Gigabyte, S1220A on ASUS) — a noticeable upgrade from most budget LGA1200 boards that came with the 10400F.

📦 Resale Value of Old Parts

Your i5-10400F + LGA1200 motherboard can be sold as a combo for ~$100–180 AUD on the used market, offsetting some of the upgrade cost. Check Gumtree/Facebook Marketplace.

🧪 Thermal Paste / Pad

The Anniversary Edition includes a Carbice Ice Pad — a durable thermal interface that doesn't need periodic repasting. If you use traditional thermal paste instead, a high-quality compound (Thermal Grizzly Kryonaut, Noctua NT-H2) is recommended given the 5800X3D's thermal sensitivity.
