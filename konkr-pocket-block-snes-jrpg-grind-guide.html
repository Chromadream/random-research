Setup on the Block

  • Folder: snes (Snes9x by default). Put patched fan translations in as their own .sfc files with an English name so ES-DE scrapes and sorts them properly.
  • Enhancement chips: Star Ocean (S-DD1) and Tengai Makyou Zero (SPC7110 plus an expanded ROM from the patch) need a current core. If one won't boot, try the bsnes core.
  • Scaling: SNES is 224 lines, so 3× = 672 tall with 24 px bars, width stretched to 4:3.
  • Fast-forward: trivial for the G85 on SNES. Map it to a shoulder for grinding.
  • Left out on purpose: Tales of Phantasia (matching the PS1 list) and Fire Emblem (covered by your separate series run).
